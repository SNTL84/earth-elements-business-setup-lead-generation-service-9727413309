# Troubleshooting

## Google Business Profile not showing on Maps

- Confirm verification is fully completed (postcard, phone, email, or video — whichever method was used).
- Check that the primary category matches the business (e.g. “Crystal Store”, “Numerologist”, “Gemstone Wholesaler”).
- Ensure the address / service area is accurate and not conflicting with another listing.
- Allow 24–72 hours after verification for full indexing.

## Social links not appearing on Google profile

- Edit the Google Business Profile → Info / Links section.
- Add the exact Instagram and Facebook URLs.
- Changes can take a few hours to propagate.

## Instagram or Facebook messaging not converting

- Ensure the accounts are set to Business / Professional.
- Add clear call-to-action (DM for consultation / price list).
- Keep branding consistent with the Google profile (logo, name, description).
